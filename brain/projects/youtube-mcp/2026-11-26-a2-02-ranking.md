## Goal and outcome

Implement the A2-02 discovery and ranking logic against the merged common contracts. Added weighted TF-IDF for videos and channels, bounded deduplicated search, fixed uploads sampling, stable ID ties and coverage. The fixture route rejects live ranking before reaching the provider while D-02 remains unresolved. Work is tracked in [issue #10](https://github.com/AadyantNeog/SEproject/issues/10) and [draft PR #11](https://github.com/AadyantNeog/SEproject/pull/11).

## What worked and what changed during verification

Hand-calculated cosine and topical mismatch cases checked the scoring independently. An initial synthetic rank test changed a video ID without updating its canonical URL. The shared contract caught the mismatch, and the fixture builder now validates the ID and URL together. Strict mypy also caught an empty tuple inferred too narrowly for a channel sample. The sample now declares its playlist occurrence type.

## Verification and remaining integration

- Passed 42 offline tests on Windows, including 11 new scoring and discovery cases. Ruff lint and formatting, strict mypy on 27 source files, and the locked wheel build passed.
- The [ranking handoff](../../../docs/developer/metadata-ranking.md) records preprocessing, default bounds and synthetic coverage. The ranking code can be reused by A2-03 and A2-04.
- J2-02 provider integration, P2-02 reservation, MCP request routing and candidate-set publication remain pending. Human owner review is pending.
