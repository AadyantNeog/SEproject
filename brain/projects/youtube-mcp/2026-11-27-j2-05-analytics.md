## Goal

Implement Jeswin's J2-05 pure library analytics engine, valid-timestamp period
comparison module, `AnalysisService` integration with Paul's `SnapshotRepository`
and Aadyant's `presentation/reports.py`, and the `analyse_library` MCP handler
on `codex/j2-05-analytics` from `origin/main` (`c5293cf`).

## Outcome

- Implemented pure analytics modules under `src/yt_intel/analytics/`:
  - `src/yt_intel/analytics/periods.py`: `TimePeriod`, `PeriodMetricComparison`,
    `UnavailableTrend`, `PeriodComparison`, `validate_non_overlapping_periods`,
    and `compare_periods`. Enforces timezone-aware UTC half-open `[start, end)`
    intervals, rejects naive, inverted, or overlapping periods, compares only
    real playlist `added_at` and subscription `subscribed_at` timestamps,
    explicitly reports like-event trends as unavailable rather than substituting
    `video.published_at`, and leaves `percent_change=None` with an explicit
    reason when the baseline count is zero.
  - `src/yt_intel/analytics/summaries.py`: `ANALYTICS_VERSION`, `AnalysisConfig`,
    `RankedBucket`, `DurationBandCount`, `RuntimeStats`,
    `LikesPopulationSummary`, `PlaylistPopulationSummary`,
    `CombinedPopulationSummary`, `SubscriptionPopulationSummary`,
    `AnalysisReport`, and `summarise_library`. Keeps likes, playlists, combined
    unique videos (with explicit overlap), and subscriptions separate; excludes
    subscription upload samples from user viewing/preference runtime stats;
    computes exact-second mean/median and the 5 SRS duration bands
    (`<5m`, `5-15m`, `15-30m`, `30-60m`, `>60m`) with explicit `retrieved`,
    `known`, and `unknown` denominators; and uses deterministic ascending ID
    tie-breaks.
  - `src/yt_intel/analytics/__init__.py`: public exports for the pure analytics
    package.
- Implemented `src/yt_intel/services/analysis.py`:
  - `AnalysisOutcome`, `build_report_sections`, and `AnalysisService`
    (`analyse_snapshot`, `analyse_stored_snapshot`), enforcing D-02 fixture-only
    policy guard, account binding isolation, 30-day retention expiry checks, and
    rendering through Aadyant's `presentation/reports.py:render_report`.
- Extended `src/yt_intel/mcp/library.py`:
  - Added the read-only `analyse_library` MCP tool accepting optional
    `snapshot_id`, `sources`, `period_a`, and `period_b` (collecting a fresh
    snapshot when `snapshot_id` is omitted) and returning `ResultEnvelope[AnalysisReport]`
    plus the rendered report text.
- Added unit and system tests:
  - `tests/unit/analytics/test_summaries.py`: verifies the SRS hand-calculated
    oracle (`[240, 600, 600, 1200]` -> mean `660`, median `600`, bands
    `[1, 2, 1, 0, 0]`, 5th unknown-duration video -> `retrieved=5, known=4, unknown=1`),
    stable tie-breaking, subscription-sample exclusion, and empty-population
    behaviour.
  - `tests/unit/analytics/test_periods.py`: verifies UTC `[start, end)` boundary
    semantics, zero-baseline `percent_change=None`, unavailable like-event trend
    without `video.published_at` substitution, and rejection of naive, inverted,
    and overlapping intervals.
  - `tests/system/test_analysis.py`: verifies `analyse_library` over real
    fixture stdio MCP with and without period comparisons, plus overlapping
    period and missing snapshot error handling.

## What worked

- Pure analytics modules (`src/yt_intel/analytics/`) require zero provider,
  database, OAuth, or approval imports.
- `AnalysisService` plugged cleanly into Paul's `SqliteSnapshotRepository` and
  Aadyant's `presentation/reports.py:render_report` without altering shared
  contracts.
